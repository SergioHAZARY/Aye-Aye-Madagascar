## What this project is

E-commerce site for **Aye-Aye Madagascar**, a Malagasy clothing brand. Next.js 16 (App Router, JavaScript, static export), built on the TemplateMo "Hexashop" template: the template's CSS, fonts, JS and layout are reused **unchanged**; the content, structure and shop features are the project's own.

The site is in **French**. All copy, component names, comments and data fields are French — keep writing them that way.

The brand's own photos are in `public/assets/images/Homme/` (9 shots of 2 garments: a red "Aye-Aye" long sleeve and an écru "Antsatsaka" long sleeve) and `public/assets/images/Femme/Femme_1.jpeg` (a beige Tarondro tee-shirt, back view, pink backdrop, 720×1280). ## Deployment

The repo is `github.com/SergioHAZARY/Aye-Aye-Madagascar` (public, at the owner's request). Every push to `main` runs `.github/workflows/deploiement-pages.yml`, which builds with `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_PATH=/Aye-Aye-Madagascar` and publishes `out/` to **https://sergiohazary.github.io/Aye-Aye-Madagascar/**. `public/.nojekyll` must stay, otherwise Pages drops the `_next` folder.

Because the site lives in a subfolder there, **every internal URL written in code goes through `chemin('/…')`** (`app/lib/chemin.js`): links, `<img>`/`<source>` paths, catalogue images, the jQuery scripts in `TemplateScripts`, stylesheets in `layout.js`, `window.location` redirects. `next.config.mjs` passes the same value to `basePath` for Next's own files. A bare `href="/x"` or `'/assets/…'` works locally and breaks online. CSS `url(...)` values are relative and need nothing.

To reproduce the online build locally from Git Bash, add `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1`, otherwise MSYS rewrites `/Aye-Aye-Madagascar` into a Windows path and `next build` rejects it.

This folder's Git identity is repo-local: author `SergioHAZARY <sergiohazary@gmail.com>`, and a repo-local credential helper that asks `gh` for the **SergioHAZARY** token. The machine's active `gh` account is a different one (SergioBZC); don't switch it.

### Adding or changing a product

Everything comes from `app/data/catalogue.js` — never hard-code a product in a page. A product is `{slug, nom, couleur, categorie, note, image, galerie[], description, details[], tailles?}`. **The site shows no price anywhere** (owner's request): no `prix` field, no amount on cards, product page, search or cart; the cart counts articles and the price is settled when the brand confirms the order. Don't add prices back unless the owner asks. Sizes come from `TAILLES_PAR_CATEGORIE` (S–XXL, 4–12 ans) unless a product sets `tailles`; read them through `taillesDuProduit()`. Those size lists are starting values still to be confirmed by the brand. The Modèles page shows them under each cut (« Tailles adulte », « Tailles enfant »), with « Disponible pour », in an info block pushed to the bottom of equal-height cards so it lines up across the three cards.

Two other lists sit in the same file, with the brand's own texts: `MODELES` (the 3 cuts, available for men, women and children) and `COLLECTIONS` (the 9 back designs, available on every cut). A collection card shows its `image` when set (all but « Madagascar » have photos) and its number otherwise; a model card (`MODELES`) shows its `image` or, without one, its SVG silhouette (all three have photos now: Femme_2, Homme_2, and for Débardeur the customer photo Client_4). `image` is the **original** photo path; pages render it through `imageCarte()`. Adding a photo means setting that one field (and, for a studio photo, adding it to `CARTES` in `photos-cadrees.mjs` and `CARTES_STUDIO` in `catalogue.js`).

`vignettes(categorie)` is the one piece of logic worth knowing: when a category has fewer than 4 products it expands each product's `galerie` into separate carousel slides, so the Homme carousel shows all 9 photos instead of 2 cards. Add a 4th Homme product and it switches to one card per product automatically.

### Cart and favourites — why no React state

`app/lib/panier.js` keeps both in `localStorage` and fires an `aye-aye:maj` event; `ActionsGlobales` installs a **single delegated click listener** on `document` for `[data-ajout-panier]` and `[data-favori]`.

This is not an accident: **owl-carousel clones the cards** for its loop, and clones are not part of the React tree, so an `onClick` on a card inside a carousel would silently do nothing. Buttons therefore carry a `data-*` attribute and no React handler. Keep it that way for anything that can appear inside a carousel. The product page is *not* in a carousel, so `GalerieProduit` and `AchatProduit` use ordinary React state and handlers.

A cart line is `{slug, taille, quantite}` and a product can sit in the cart once per size. A card's cart icon adds with `taille: null`; `PanierDetail` then shows a size `<select>` on that line and refuses to validate the order until every sized product has one. The product page adds with the chosen size, and its button stays grey until a size is picked.

Read paths (`CompteurPanier`, `PanierDetail`, `Favoris`) are normal client components that subscribe to the event. They render nothing on the server (`useState(null)`) because the cart only exists in the browser — don't "fix" that, it prevents a hydration mismatch.

### The jQuery layer

`TemplateScripts` injects, in order and after hydration: jQuery, owl-carousel, scrollReveal, then `custom.js` and `aye-aye.js`. These five are the only files left in `public/assets/js/`: the template's other 11 scripts (984 KB in all), `flex-slider.css`, `lightbox.css`, the slick/flexslider/Flaticon fonts, Font Awesome's `.eot/.svg/.ttf/.otf` (modern browsers take the `.woff2`) and the template images nothing used were deleted from the project (owner's request: as little code as possible). **Don't re-add a vendor file unless something calls it.**

`script.async = false` guarantees execution order, a module flag prevents a double injection under StrictMode, and `jQuery(window).trigger('load')` is replayed when `readyState === 'complete'` for `custom.js`'s load handlers. The scripts must run after hydration (owl moves and clones nodes React owns), but `layout.js` preloads them with `<link rel="preload" as="script">` so they download in parallel with Next's JS; the list lives in `app/lib/scripts-template.js` for both.

`public/assets/js/aye-aye.js` is the project's own script: it turns the home header opaque after the banner, and removes `custom.js`'s broken scroll-spy with `$(document).off('scroll')` inside a `$(function(){})` — the `ready` queue matters, custom.js's callback runs *after* this file loads.

### Navigation is full page loads, on purpose

Internal links are plain `<a href="/homme/">`, not `next/link`. With client-side navigation React swaps the DOM while jQuery keeps handlers on the old nodes: carousels would not re-init and the burger menu would lose its handler. `ActionsGlobales` softens the cost of full loads: hovering or touching an internal link inserts a `<link rel="prefetch">` for it (skipped under Save-Data). Switch a page to `next/link` only once it no longer depends on `custom.js`.

## Styling rules

**Content width**: from 1200 px every `.container` is `max-width: max(1140px, min(1620px, 86%))` (owner's request: all sections, header to footer, wider than Bootstrap's 1140 px). The home banner text uses the same expression in its `left` so it stays aligned on the logo; change both together.

**`public/assets/css/aye-aye.css` is the only stylesheet to edit.** The template's remaining stylesheets (`bootstrap.min.css`, `font-awesome.css`, `templatemo-hexashop.css`) are byte-identical to the original and must stay that way; the project sheet is loaded last. It uses only the template's palette (`#2a2a2a`, `#f7f7f7`, `#eee`) and Poppins — no new font, no new colour. One exception, asked for by the owner: the Facebook logo of the « Continuer avec Facebook » button on `/connexion/` sits in a round Facebook-blue badge (`#1877f2`, `.aye-icone-facebook`) so it is recognisable at a glance.

Three things in it are load-bearing:

- **`img[width][height] { height: auto }`** — every `<img>` carries its intrinsic `width`/`height` so the browser reserves the exact space before the file arrives (measured CLS ≈ 0). Without this rule the `height` attribute acts as a fixed height and **stretches every image**. Note its specificity (0,2,1): a rule like `.aye-logo img` loses to it, which is why the logo rules are written `.header-area .aye-logo img[width][height]`.
- **Generic `.item` card rules.** The template only styled product cards under `#products`, `#men`, `#women`, `#kids`. The project's sections use other ids, so the same rules are repeated there without an id. A new product section gets them for free; don't re-scope them to an id.
- **Header height.** The header is 120 px on desktop (template: 100) so the vertical logo stays legible; `.main-banner`, `.page-heading`, `.about-page-heading` and the product page's `.aye-fiche` are pushed down to match, so the gap under the header stays the template's 60 px. Sticky is the template's 80 px. On mobile the header is 64 px (see below). Change one and you must change the others.
- **Buttons on light backgrounds.** The template's `.main-border-button` is white, made for dark photos, and was invisible on the project's white sections. The containers listed in the « Boutons sur fond clair » rule get the template's own dark variant (the one from its Explore section). Add any new light-background button container to that rule; `.aye-plein` gives the filled primary button.

## Performance

The site is used on slow Malagasy mobile connections (measured from here: ~70 KB/s, ~0.7 s per request to GitHub Pages). Rules that keep it fast:

- **No preloader.** The template's `#preloader` hid the page until window `load`, i.e. until the last file arrived: 6 s to 100 s+ online while the content was ready at 2–3 s. It was removed (owner's complaint that the site « rame »); don't bring it back.
- **Carousels are visible before owl.** `owl-carousel.css` sets `.owl-carousel { display: none }` until init; `aye-aye.css` (« Carrousels avant owl-carousel ») lays the cards out 1/2/3 per row with 30 px gaps, at owl's 600/1000 px breakpoints, so nothing jumps when owl starts.
- **Images declare their real size.** `dimensionsImage()` in `catalogue.js` gives width/height (brand photos 899×1600, template 370×390 plus listed exceptions). A wrong ratio made cards grow when the photo arrived. Add an entry when a catalogue image has another size.
- **Brand photos are served at the size shown**: `photoReduite(src, 160|480|720)` and `srcsetPhoto(src)` pick from `Homme/reduites/` and `Femme/reduites/` (only widths below the original; `PHOTOS_MARQUE` in `catalogue.js` holds each folder's size), made by `node scripts/photos-reduites.mjs`. Re-run it after adding or replacing a photo. The original is used only for the large product photo on high-density screens and for the zoom link.
- **Service worker** (`public/sw.js`, registered by `ActionsGlobales` in production builds only): GitHub Pages sends `max-age=600`, so after 10 minutes every page re-requested its ~25 files (~0.7 s round trip each from Madagascar). The worker keeps them: pages network-first with a 2.5 s fallback to the stored copy (updates show at once, the site still opens offline), `_next/static` cache-first, other files and Google Fonts stale-while-revalidate. It is registered as `sw.js?v=<build version>` and its caches are named after that version, so each deploy installs a new worker that empties the previous caches. `aye-aye.css` and `aye-aye.js` are linked with the same `?v=` (`cheminVersionne()` in `app/lib/chemin.js`; the version comes from `next.config.mjs`, `GITHUB_SHA` online). Measured: return visit after cache expiry 288 ms and 0 requests with the worker, 1.7 s and 53 requests without. Kill switch: publish a `sw.js` that calls `self.registration.unregister()`.
- **Prefetch**: the 5 menu pages are prefetched once the page is idle (not on Save-Data or 2G), any internal link on hover/touch. The hero image has `fetchPriority="high"`.
- **No cross-document View Transitions**: `@view-transition { navigation: auto }` was tried and every navigation logged « Transition was aborted … opt-in disabled »; it was removed.
- Measured after these changes (mobile, 1.6 Mbit/s, 150 ms, CPU ×4): content painted at 1.3–2.1 s, CLS ≈ 0, Homme page 447 KB (was 641), product page 440 KB (was 1056).

Every image derived from the brand photos (banner, its panoramic desktop, tablet and portrait mobile versions, heading strips, pillars, « Notre histoire », the 3:4 card versions) comes from `node scripts/photos-cadrees.mjs`. The script fits the whole portrait photo in the frame and fills the gap by stretching a 1 px edge strip, blurred along its length. The studio backdrop is plain grey, so it continues without a seam. The strip is 1 px because a wider one catches a sleeve near the edge once the photo is shrunk, and the blur is there because unblurred JPEG grain turns into thin lines when stretched. Don't feed it `DSC_1806`: the raised arm touches the left edge and would be smeared. A photo whose subject touches an edge can still be used with `bordsPropres: true` (pixels that differ from the surrounding backdrop are replaced by it before stretching) and `retrait: 2` (the strip is taken 2 px in, and the fill covers the photo's darker last 2 px): that is how Femme_1, whose right sleeve touches the edge, is framed. A source may be given relative to `images/` (`'Femme/Femme_1.jpeg'`). For outdoor photos that must become wider (banner slides 2 and 3), `fondu: 90` builds the frame from smooth gradients taken from the photo's edges (the strip is averaged along its length, so the scenery leaves no streaks) and lays the whole photo on top, faded into them over 90 px on the sides and the top. Two sharp pitfalls found doing it: `removeAlpha()` runs after `joinChannel()` in a pipeline and strips the mask (call it in a separate pipeline), and `extend({ extendWith: 'copy' })` leaves black here (extend by hand). Outdoor photos (garden, red gate, city at dusk) can't be edge-stretched, and **no blur fill**: the owner rejected blurred sides. ## Images

Brand photos come out of the camera at 3400×6048 and ~1 MB each. `npm run images` compresses anything over 500 KB (resize to 1600 px, JPEG q80) and keeps untouched copies in `photos-originales/`; template images stay below the threshold and are never touched. Everything in `public/assets/images/{banniere,piliers,apropos,cartes}/` is generated by `scripts/photos-cadrees.mjs` (see Gotchas): edit the script, not the files.
