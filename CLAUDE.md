## Deployment

The repo is `github.com/SergioHAZARY/Aye-Aye-Madagascar` (public, at the owner's request). Every push to `main` runs `.github/workflows/deploiement-pages.yml`, which builds with `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_PATH=/Aye-Aye-Madagascar` and publishes `out/` to **https://sergiohazary.github.io/Aye-Aye-Madagascar/**. `public/.nojekyll` must stay, otherwise Pages drops the `_next` folder.

Because the site lives in a subfolder there, **every internal URL written in code goes through `chemin('/…')`** (`app/lib/chemin.js`): links, `<img>`/`<source>` paths, catalogue images, the script injected by `TemplateScripts`, stylesheets in `layout.js`, `window.location` redirects. `next.config.mjs` passes the same value to `basePath` for Next's own files. A bare `href="/x"` or `'/assets/…'` works locally and breaks online. CSS `url(...)` values are relative and need nothing.

To reproduce the online build locally from Git Bash, add `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1`, otherwise MSYS rewrites `/Aye-Aye-Madagascar` into a Windows path and `next build` rejects it.

This folder's Git identity is repo-local: author `SergioHAZARY <sergiohazary@gmail.com>`, and a repo-local credential helper that asks `gh` for the **SergioHAZARY** token. The machine's active `gh` account is a different one (SergioBZC); don't switch it.

### Adding or changing a product

Everything comes from `app/data/catalogue.js` — never hard-code a product in a page. A product is `{slug, nom, couleur, categorie, modele, collection, image, galerie[], description, tailles?}`; `modele` and `collection` are slugs of `MODELES` / `COLLECTIONS` and tie the product to the gallery. There is one product per garment shown in the galleries (19: the 3 packshot-only ones are listed under « Packshots »; the 7 added for gallery photos are Tee-shirt Lemurs of Madagascar, Tee-shirt Baobab, Débardeur Antsatsaka / Djembe / Lemurs of Madagascar (Homme) and Tee-shirt Aye-Aye Madagascar, Tee-shirt Madagascar (Femme), each with one photo copied from `photos-originales/nouvelles/` into `Homme/` or `Femme/`: DSC_1798, DSC_1802, Homme_3 (IMG_1565), Homme_5 (IMG_1623, a group: no customer-free photo shows the Baobab tee alone), DSC_1836, DSC_1842). No stars, no `note` (owner's request). **The site shows no price anywhere** (owner's request): no `prix` field, no amount on cards, product page, search or cart; the cart counts articles and the price is settled when the brand confirms the order. Don't add prices back unless the owner asks. Sizes come from `TAILLES_PAR_CATEGORIE` (S–XXL, 2–12 ans) unless a product sets `tailles`; read them through `taillesDuProduit()`. Those size lists are starting values still to be confirmed by the brand. The Modèles page shows them under each cut (« Tailles adulte », « Tailles enfant »), with « Disponible pour », in an info block pushed to the bottom of equal-height cards so it lines up across the three cards.

Two other lists sit in the same file, with the brand's own texts: `MODELES` (the 3 cuts, available for men, women and children) and `COLLECTIONS` (the 10 back designs, available on every cut; « Baobab », the 10th, has a provisional text of ours until the owner sends theirs). A collection card shows its `image` when set (all but « Madagascar » and « Baobab »; they show their first gallery photo) and its number otherwise; a model card (`MODELES`) shows its `image` or, without one, its SVG silhouette (all three have photos now: Femme_2, Homme_2, and for Débardeur the customer photo Client_4). `image` is the **original** photo path; pages render it through `imageCarte()`. Adding a photo means setting that one field (and, for a studio photo, adding it to `CARTES` in `photos-cadrees.mjs` and `CARTES_STUDIO` in `catalogue.js`).

`vignettes(categorie)` is the one piece of logic worth knowing: when a category has fewer than 4 products it expands each product's `galerie` into separate carousel slides, so the Homme carousel shows all 9 photos instead of 2 cards. Add a 4th Homme product and it switches to one card per product automatically.

### Cart and favourites — why no React state

`app/lib/panier.js` keeps both in `localStorage` and fires an `aye-aye:maj` event; `ActionsGlobales` installs a **single delegated click listener** on `document` for `[data-ajout-panier]` and `[data-favori]`.

This dates from the template carousels (gone now, with jQuery): **owl-carousel cloned the cards** for its loop, and clones are not part of the React tree, so an `onClick` on a card inside a carousel would silently do nothing. Buttons therefore carry a `data-*` attribute and no React handler. Keep it that way for anything that can appear inside a carousel. The product page is *not* in a carousel, so `GalerieProduit` and `AchatProduit` use ordinary React state and handlers.

A cart line is `{slug, taille, quantite}` and a product can sit in the cart once per size. A card's cart icon adds with `taille: null`; `PanierDetail` then shows a size `<select>` on that line and refuses to validate the order until every sized product has one. The product page no longer has an add-to-cart button (owner's request), so nothing in the site adds to the cart any more; the cart code stays for when ordering is wired.

Read paths (`CompteurPanier`, `PanierDetail`, `Favoris`) are normal client components that subscribe to the event. They render nothing on the server (`useState(null)`) because the cart only exists in the browser — don't "fix" that, it prevents a hydration mismatch.

### The project script (no jQuery any more)

`public/assets/js/aye-aye.js` is the **only script outside Next**, plain JavaScript. The template's whole stack (jQuery, owl-carousel, scrollReveal, `custom.js`: 182 KB, 4 requests) was removed: the site only used two of its behaviours, both rewritten identically in `aye-aye.js`: the sticky header (`background-header` once the `#top` block is scrolled past, or at the first scroll on pages without `#top`, exactly as `custom.js` computed it) and the phone menu (`.menu-trigger` toggles `active` on itself and `aye-menu-ouvert` on `ul.nav`; the opening animation is CSS). **Don't bring jQuery or a template script back.**

`TemplateScripts` injects it after hydration (it only sets classes on elements React has just taken over) and `layout.js` preloads it. It also does: `aye-entete-opaque` (transparent header turns white after the banner), the « back to top » button (`.aye-haut`, in `Footer.js`, shown after 1.5 screens), and all scroll animations (see « Motion »).

### Motion (one grammar for the whole site)

Last block of `aye-aye.css`, « MOUVEMENT ET FINITIONS ». Only `opacity` and `transform` move, so nothing shifts the layout.
- **Blocks rise into place once**, the first time they enter the screen: `aye-aye.js` adds `aye-apparait` to the list of selectors at the top of its « Apparition des blocs » section (section titles, product / model / collection cards, univers cards, points forts, about and contact blocks…), then `aye-vu`; blocks arriving together are staggered 90 ms (`--aye-delai`). **Anything on screen at load is left alone**: no flash, the first view paints as fast as before. A new kind of block joins the animation by adding its selector to that list.
- The « Nos collections » rows and the gallery photos keep their own slide-in, replayed every time (owner's choice).
- Banner titles (`.inner-content > *`) rise at page load, pure CSS. Measured on a phone, cold cache, 5 runs with and without: same LCP, same CLS.
- Hover: card photos scale 1.04 inside their frame, card names get a growing underline, menu links a thin centred underline (also the active link), gallery photos that lead to a product show a round arrow, footer links step 4 px, points forts icons lift.
- Product page: the main photo fades when a thumbnail is chosen (`key` on the `<img>`).
- `html { scroll-behavior: smooth }` with `scroll-margin-top` for anchors, one `:focus-visible` outline everywhere, and a global `prefers-reduced-motion` rule that switches every animation and transition off. In test scripts, scroll with `behavior: 'instant'`.

### Navigation is full page loads, on purpose

Internal links are plain `<a href="/homme/">`, not `next/link`. `aye-aye.js` runs once per page load and binds to the elements present (header, menu, blocks to animate): with client-side navigation React would swap the DOM under it. `ActionsGlobales` softens the cost of full loads: hovering or touching an internal link inserts a `<link rel="prefetch">` for it (skipped under Save-Data). Moving to `next/link` would mean re-running that script's setup on every route change.

## Styling rules

**Content width**: from 1200 px every `.container` is `max-width: max(1140px, min(1620px, 86%))` (owner's request: all sections, header to footer, wider than Bootstrap's 1140 px). The home banner text uses the same expression in its `left` so it stays aligned on the logo; change both together.

**`public/assets/css/aye-aye.css` is the only stylesheet to edit.** The template's remaining stylesheets (`bootstrap.min.css`, `font-awesome.css`, `templatemo-hexashop.css`) are byte-identical to the original and must stay that way; the project sheet is loaded last. It uses only the template's palette (`#2a2a2a`, `#f7f7f7`, `#eee`) and Poppins — no new font, no new colour. One exception, asked for by the owner: the Facebook logo of the « Continuer avec Facebook » button on `/connexion/` sits in a round Facebook-blue badge (`#1877f2`, `.aye-icone-facebook`) so it is recognisable at a glance.

Three things in it are load-bearing:

- **`img[width][height] { height: auto }`** — every `<img>` carries its intrinsic `width`/`height` so the browser reserves the exact space before the file arrives (measured CLS ≈ 0). Without this rule the `height` attribute acts as a fixed height and **stretches every image**. Note its specificity (0,2,1): a rule like `.aye-logo img` loses to it, which is why the logo rules are written `.header-area .aye-logo img[width][height]`.
- **Generic `.item` card rules.** The template only styled product cards under `#products`, `#men`, `#women`, `#kids`. The project's sections use other ids, so the same rules are repeated there without an id. A new product section gets them for free; don't re-scope them to an id.

## Performance

The site is used on slow Malagasy mobile connections (measured from here: ~70 KB/s, ~0.7 s per request to GitHub Pages). Rules that keep it fast:

- **No preloader.** The template's `#preloader` hid the page until window `load`, i.e. until the last file arrived: 6 s to 100 s+ online while the content was ready at 2–3 s. It was removed (owner's complaint that the site « rame »); don't bring it back.
- **182 KB of template JavaScript removed** (jQuery, owl-carousel, scrollReveal, custom.js): one 6 KB project script instead of five files.
- **Images declare their real size.** `dimensionsImage()` in `catalogue.js` gives width/height (brand photos 899×1600, template 370×390 plus listed exceptions). A wrong ratio made cards grow when the photo arrived. Add an entry when a catalogue image has another size.
- **Brand photos are served at the size shown**: `photoReduite(src, 160|480|720)` and `srcsetPhoto(src)` pick from `Homme/reduites/` and `Femme/reduites/` (only widths below the original; `PHOTOS_MARQUE` in `catalogue.js` holds each folder's size), made by `node scripts/photos-reduites.mjs`. Re-run it after adding or replacing a photo. The original is used only for the large product photo on high-density screens and for the zoom link.
- **Service worker** (`public/sw.js`, registered by `ActionsGlobales` in production builds only): GitHub Pages sends `max-age=600`, so after 10 minutes every page re-requested its ~25 files (~0.7 s round trip each from Madagascar). The worker keeps them: pages network-first with a 2.5 s fallback to the stored copy (updates show at once, the site still opens offline), `_next/static` cache-first, other files and Google Fonts stale-while-revalidate. It is registered as `sw.js?v=<build version>` and its caches are named after that version, so each deploy installs a new worker that empties the previous caches. `aye-aye.css` and `aye-aye.js` are linked with the same `?v=` (`cheminVersionne()` in `app/lib/chemin.js`; the version comes from `next.config.mjs`, `GITHUB_SHA` online). Measured: return visit after cache expiry 288 ms and 0 requests with the worker, 1.7 s and 53 requests without. Kill switch: publish a `sw.js` that calls `self.registration.unregister()`.
- **Prefetch**: the 5 menu pages are prefetched once the page is idle (not on Save-Data or 2G), any internal link on hover/touch. The hero image has `fetchPriority="high"`.
- **No cross-document View Transitions**: `@view-transition { navigation: auto }` was tried and every navigation logged « Transition was aborted … opt-in disabled »; it was removed.
- Measured after these changes (mobile, 1.6 Mbit/s, 150 ms, CPU ×4): content painted at 1.3–2.1 s, CLS ≈ 0, Homme page 447 KB (was 641), product page 440 KB (was 1056).
